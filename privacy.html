Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

Housing Hues, Klerksdorp, North West, South Africa. We are the responsible party for what you send through this site.

What we collect

Request and Get in on it forms: your name, your phone, WhatsApp or email, and whatever you type (service, timeline, budget, craft, link, message).

Artist suggestions: the artist name you type. Nothing else.

Reactions: a heart or thumbs-down. No personal details.

Visit counts: anonymous page-view numbers from Vercel Web Analytics.

Why

To reply to you and deal with your request. Nothing else.

Who sees it

Only Housing Hues can read what you submit. We don't sell it and we don't use it for marketing lists.

How long we keep it

For as long as it takes to deal with your request, and no longer than we need it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you at any time. Email housinghues@gmail.com and we'll sort it.

If you're not happy with how we handled it, you can complain to South Africa's Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.
